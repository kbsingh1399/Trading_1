1 Executive Summary


HD-Intel is a live wallet-flow intelligence platform that answers one operational question:
 
on which of the


347 Hyperliquid markets are the wallets that matter moving money right now, and in which direction?
 
The


system ingests the complete Hyperdash cohort analytics surface together with the public Hyperliquid market


feed, distills it into eleven per-cohort positioning series per market, and converts those series into seven


families of tradeable signals plus a composite 0-100 Intelligence Score that ranks every asset by smart-money


conviction.


This document is the complete blueprint for the platform: the data-source survey that maps every verified


API operation and its latency, the intelligence model with exact feature and scoring formulas, the five-layer


system architecture, the ingestion design with tiered refresh and rate-budgeted politeness, storage schemas,


the serving contract, a Docker Compose deployment topology with a documented scale-out path, security


and operations runbooks, and - critically - a live validation section where every number quoted in this paper


was produced by the actual reference implementation running against production data.

The reference stack is deliberately small: three containers (ClickHouse, a Python FastAPI backend that


embeds its own ingestion supervisor, and a Next.js dashboard) start with a single command and sustain a


one-minute intelligence cycle on a commodity VPS. Every design decision trades elegance for operability -


in-process hot state instead of a message broker, bounded politeness instead of raw throughput, honest


warm-up semantics instead of fabricated history - while section 9 documents exactly which seams to pull


when volume grows ten-fold.


2 Problem Statement and Objectives


Hyperdash visualizes wallet cohorts on Hyperliquid, but the question our desk needs answered is not “what


am I looking at” - it is “where should I be looking, and which way.” That requires continuously computing


how every cohort's positioning changes on every market, comparing elite-wallet behavior against crowd


behavior, detecting risk formations before they unwind, and surfacing the handful of assets each hour where


positioning actually disagrees with price. None of that exists as a deployable system; it exists as clicking


through a web UI.

O1 - Coverage.
 
All ~350 tradable markets on Hyperliquid (crypto, equities, indices, commodities,


FX), refreshed on a tiered schedule, with the top-20 by volume receiving full per-wallet position books.
